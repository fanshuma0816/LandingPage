✿ good things to know

A few gentle answers.

How long is one lesson? +
Around 20 to 90 minutes, depending on the text. Lessons stay small on purpose so you can finish in one sitting.
What can I bring? +
A short piece you already like: an article, transcript, newsletter, book excerpt, interview, or dialogue. Around 400 to 1,000 characters works best.
Does it read the text aloud? +
Yes. Lingua reads your whole text aloud in a natural voice, so you can listen, read along, and shadow it.
How is this different from a tutor? +
No booking or waiting, a fraction of the cost, and a coach tuned to your exact level — ready whenever you are, as often as you like.
Is it free right now? +
Yes. You can try a full guided lesson for free. Paid packs arrive later, when the beta opens.
