A few gentle answers.

How long is one lesson?
Around 10 to 30 minutes of focused attention, depending on the text. Lessons stay small on purpose so you can finish in one sitting.
What can I bring?
A short piece you already like: an article, transcript, newsletter, book excerpt, textbook page, interview, or dialogue. Around 400 to 1,000 characters works best.
Does it read the text aloud?
Yes. Lingua reads your whole text aloud in a natural voice, so you can listen, read along, and shadow it sentence by sentence.
How is this different from a tutor?
No booking or waiting, a fraction of the cost, and a tutor tuned to your exact level — ready whenever you are, as often as you like.
Is it free right now?
Yes. You can try a full guided lesson for free. Paid packs arrive later, as the beta opens up.
